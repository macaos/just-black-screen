# Just Black Screen

A macOS menu bar app that covers every screen in black, so your Mac looks asleep while it keeps working.
Move the mouse and a PIN pad appears. Enter your 4-digit PIN to get back to work.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/macaos/just-black-screen/releases/latest/download/JustBlackScreen.dmg)**
· macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

- Try every feature free for 7 days. No account and no card.
- After that, $4.99 once. One license covers up to 3 Macs.
  [Buy a license](https://just-black-screen.web.app/r/github)
- The app updates itself. To check right away, choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar icon.
